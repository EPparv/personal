About

It started with Bitcoin.

I found crypto at 12 while looking for a way to get paid. Eventually, that led me to building Estate Protocol.

The short version

At 12, I made Zynga Poker’s national leaderboard, and people started asking to buy in-game chips for real money. I was too young to open a bank account, so I went looking for another way to get paid and found Bitcoin.

I sold chips for BTC, then sold the BTC for cash through P2P marketplaces like LocalBitcoins. Later, I traded the price differences between Indian and international crypto markets and wrote about security token offerings for WazirX.

Building Estate

With Estate Protocol, I brought together property inventory, a tokenization platform on Arbitrum, and the legal structure needed to distribute the offerings. I personally closed the partnerships and worked across product, compliance, fundraising, and integrations.

We originated $13M in property supply and listed 26+ properties. About 300 investors funded purchases. I raised $550K in investment capital and secured a separate $100K Arbitrum Foundation grant.

I led 14 people over the company’s history, with up to 11 working together at once. I also served as a judge and mentor for Arbitrum Founder House programs in Bengaluru and New York City.

Outside work

I read widely, still enjoy a game with asymmetric information, and spend a lot of time following the people and ideas shaping tokenization, DeFi, and stablecoins.
